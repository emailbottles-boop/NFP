Mission Statement

Why We Exist

We strive to breathe magic back into this world in the strangest ways possible. Our goal is to educate while sparking (up) curiosity, laughter, and a sense of wonder that lingers long after the lesson ends.

Vision Statement

Where We're Going

Substance use has always been part of the human story. The stigma around it hasn't helped anyone — it's kept people silent, kept them from asking for help, and locked the conversation in spaces most people never enter.

We're changing that by changing the medium. Not a pamphlet. Not a hotline. Not a classroom.

A form of media that can reach adults across the world.

Method

How We Do It

Entertainment is our method. Harm reduction is our mission. The show exists because the mission exists — not the other way around.

Our animated world is one where substances manifest as powers — seductive, corrupting, and real — told through characters people love and stories that reflect the true human experience of drug use without judgment, and reaching audiences with the knowledge to navigate it.
