Who We Are

Madam Wizzy

Madam Wizzy is a harm reduction organization using original adult animation to reach people who have never sought help — through characters they love and stories that feel real because they are.

We have a board of directors actively applying for nonprofit status in Washington State, with the goal of expanding our work across the United States.

The mission is harm reduction. The method is entertainment. The work saves lives.

What We're Building

A Show That Does Something Real

We're producing original adult animation set in a world where substances manifest as powers — seductive, corrupting, real. Not a lecture. Not a PSA. A world people want to live in that teaches them something true while they're there.

Reducing the stigma around drug use isn't a side effect of what we do. It's the whole point. And we fund it through wizardshit.store — owned and operated by us, 100% of proceeds back into the mission.
