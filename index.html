Who We Are

WIZ@RD SHIT

We create original adult animation built around one mission: harm reduction. Our world is one where substances manifest as powers — seductive, corrupting, and real — told through characters people love and stories that reflect the true human experience of drug use without judgment.

We don't lecture. We don't preach. We tell stories. And when the story ends, something changes.

Madam Wizzy WA NFP is the organization behind the work. wizardshit.store funds it. The show is the mission.
