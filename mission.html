Our Mission

Entertainment is our method. Harm reduction is our mission.

Mission Statement

Why We Exist

We strive to breathe magic back into this world in the strangest ways possible. Our goal is to educate while sparking (up) curiosity, laughter, and a sense of wonder that lingers long after the lesson ends.

The Method

Entertainment First. Always.

Our animated world was founded to represent real issues facing society — where substances manifest as powers that seduce, corrupt, and wreak havoc as commodities — reflecting the true human experience of drug use without judgment, and reaching audiences with the knowledge to navigate it.

The show exists because the mission exists. Not the other way around.

Who We Reach

Anyone

People in recovery who want to see their story told without shame. People who've never thought about harm reduction and find it through something they actually enjoy. People who love someone struggling. People who are struggling. People who got through it.

Animation reaches anyone. That's why we chose it.

The Tagline

High Magic, Higher Minds.

Magic is the world we built. Higher minds is the world we're building toward. The two aren't separate — the story is the lesson, and the lesson is the story.
