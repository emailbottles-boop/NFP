Our Mission

Entertainment is our method. Harm reduction is our mission.

Mission Statement

Why We Exist

We are the first harm reduction organization using original adult animation to reach people who have never sought out harm reduction — through a character they love, in stories that reflect the real human experience of substance use without judgment.

We strive to breathe magic back into this world in the strangest ways possible. Our goal is to educate while sparking curiosity, laughter, and a sense of wonder that lingers long after the lesson ends.

The Method

Entertainment First. Always.

Our animated world was founded to represent real issues facing society — where substances manifest as powers that seduce, corrupt, and wreak havoc as commodities — reflecting the true human experience of drug use without judgment, and reaching audiences with the knowledge to navigate it.

The show exists because the mission exists. Not the other way around.

Who We Reach

The People No One Else Gets To

People who have never Googled harm reduction.

People who would never walk into a resource center.

People who would watch a show, fall in love with a character, and learn something real — without ever feeling like they were being taught.

We reach them through story. Through humor. Through a world that feels true because it is.
